How Awaaz Works

From citizen report to municipal repair in four seamless steps.

STEP 01

Snap & Upload

Capture a clear photograph of the damaged road section and submit it on Awaaz.

STEP 02

AI Analysis

Deep vision model processes image features, assigns severity score, and generates complaint text.

STEP 03

Community Upvote

Published to public civic feed where community members upvote and validate the report.

STEP 04

Municipal Action

Public Works Department receives prioritized tickets and executes targeted road repairs.
